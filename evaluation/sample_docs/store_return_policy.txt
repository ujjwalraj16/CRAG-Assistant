Store Return and Refund Policy

Eligibility Windows:
- Unopened electronics may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Opened laptops and tablets have a 15-day return window when returned with all original accessories.
- Defective items qualify for free replacement or refund with a $0 restocking fee.

Required Documentation:
- Every return or warranty exchange requires the original purchase receipt and matching product serial number.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after inspection.
