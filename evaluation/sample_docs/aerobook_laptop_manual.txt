AeroBook Pro 14 Product Manual

Hardware Specifications:
- Display: 14-inch 2.8K OLED anti-glare panel, 400 nits brightness.
- Memory & Storage: 16GB LPDDR5 RAM and 512GB NVMe SSD.
- Weight: 1.28 kg aluminum unibody chassis.
- Ports: 1x HDMI 2.1 output port, 2x Thunderbolt 4 USB-C ports, 1x 3.5mm audio jack.
- Battery & Charging: 14-hour video playback battery life; includes a 65W USB-C fast charger in the box.

Warranty Coverage:
- The AeroBook Pro 14 includes a 2-year battery warranty and standard hardware defect coverage from the original purchase date.
- Warranty service requires the original purchase receipt and device serial number.
