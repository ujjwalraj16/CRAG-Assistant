FinServe High-Yield Savings Account FAQ

Account Terms:
- Annual Percentage Yield (APY): 4.25% variable APY compounded daily and credited monthly.
- Monthly Maintenance Fee: $0 monthly fee and no minimum opening balance requirement.
- Deposit Insurance: Balances are FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor.
- Withdrawal Limits: Up to 6 fee-free electronic withdrawals or transfers per statement cycle.
