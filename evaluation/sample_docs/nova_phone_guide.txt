Nova X5 Smartphone User Guide

Key Specifications:
- Camera System: 50MP main sensor with optical image stabilization (OIS) and 12MP ultrawide lens.
- Display: 6.5-inch 120Hz AMOLED screen with Gorilla Glass protection.
- Durability: IP68 dust and water immersion resistance (up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes).
- Battery: 4500mAh capacity supporting 45W wired charging.

Warranty Information:
- Nova X5 carries a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering factory hardware defects.
- Liquid damage beyond IP68 certification limits and cracked screens are excluded from standard warranty coverage.
